# Acme Home Goods - Customer Support Policies

## Shipping
- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days of being placed.
- Standard shipping (free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99) arrives 3-7 business days after shipping.
- Express shipping ($14.99) arrives 1-2 business days after shipping.
- We ship within the United States only.
- Tracking numbers are emailed when an order ships and are shown in the order record.
- If a shipped order shows no tracking movement for 7 or more days, a team member opens a carrier investigation. The agent should tell the customer a team member will follow up.

## Order changes and cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled or have their shipping address changed only while their status is "processing". A team member makes the change; the agent cannot.
- Orders with status "shipped" or "delivered" cannot be cancelled or redirected; the customer can return them after delivery.

## Returns
- Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- To start a return, the customer visits https://acmehomegoods.example/returns and enters their order ID and email. A prepaid return label is emailed within 1 business day.
- Return shipping is free for defective or damaged items. For all other returns, $6.95 is deducted from the refund for the return label.
- Final-sale items (marked final_sale in the order record) cannot be returned.
- Returns requested more than 30 days after delivery need approval from a team member.

## Refunds
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received at our warehouse.
- Any refund or credit outside the standard return process (for example goodwill credits, price adjustments, or refunds without a return) needs approval from a team member. The agent must not promise one.

## Damaged, defective, or wrong items
- Customers should reply with a photo of the damage or the wrong item and the order ID.
- Once a team member reviews the photo (within 1 business day), we ship a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.
- Damage must be reported within 14 days of delivery.

## Warranty
- All furniture carries a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects from the delivery date. Warranty claims are reviewed by a team member.
